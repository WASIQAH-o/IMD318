Experience Page

I have gained valuable academic experience in managing information through what I have learned at UiTM Rembau. During my studies, I was exposed to various aspects of information management, such as the procedures for storing library materials and archival record-keeping.

I also had the opportunity to participate in an educational visit to SIRIM, where my classmates and I conducted interviews to learn how they store and manage all their records, both in digital and physical formats, including the procedures used in their filing rooms.

My Diploma program has taught me important skills such as coding, cataloging, and organizing information systems. Before university, I studied Arabic language subjects during my secondary school years, which helped me develop communication skills.

Besides academic learning, I also have some work experience as a promoter, where I was paid RM150 per day. Although the job was tiring, I enjoyed it because it helped me gain confidence and improve my communication skills.

During my industrial training at UiTM Seremban, under the Islamic Affairs Unit, I worked as an assistant for event coordination, helped with VIP seating and tagging arrangements, and prepared annual program reports. Although some tasks were quite challenging, I felt proud and happy to complete them successfully.

Family Page

My family consists of seven members my mother, father, eldest sister, two older brothers, myself, and my youngest brother. My father is a businessman, while my mother is a wonderful homemaker who takes great care of all of us.

My eldest sister is intelligent and loving; my second brother is firm yet gentle; my third brother is caring and considerate; and my youngest brother is mature and responsible. My family is truly harmonious and close-knit.

My father is very disciplined and always reminds us to be mindful of our diet, studies, and manners. Both my parents encourage us to work hard and be people who bring goodness and benefit to others. I am truly grateful to grow up in such a loving and supportive family.

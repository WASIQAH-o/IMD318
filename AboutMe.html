Biodata Page

My name is Wasiqah binti Rosli, and I was born in Selangor on February 7, 2005, at exactly 7:55 a.m. at the Kota Damansara Women’s Specialist Centre in Petaling Jaya. I am the fourth child out of five siblings, making me the second youngest in my family.

Currently, I am continuing my studies at UiTM Rembau, pursuing a Diploma in Information Management. I am now in my final semester, and I feel both excited and a little sad, knowing that I only have a few months left here.

I would describe myself as 50% introvert and 50% extrovert — quiet most of the time, but cheerful and talkative when I’m with my close friends. During my free time, I enjoy online shopping and singing. I also love watching action, horror, and drama movies.

Before entering university, I studied at Sekolah Agama Menengah Paya Jaras from Form 1 until Form 5, where I learned many valuable lessons and made unforgettable memories.

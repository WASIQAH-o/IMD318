Education Page

My education journey began when I attended Q-Dess Kindergarten at the age of six. During my time there, I learned to recite the pledge in both English and Chinese — and I still remember the Chinese version today!

I then started my primary education at Sekolah Kebangsaan Sungai Buloh, while also attending Sekolah Rendah Agama Zahidiah for my religious studies.

Later, I continued my secondary education at Sekolah Agama Menengah Paya Jaras, where I developed more discipline and leadership skills.

I also served as a school prefect, participated in poetry recitation, public speaking, and storytelling competitions, and was active in sports like running, handball, and netball. These experiences shaped me into a more confident and active student.
